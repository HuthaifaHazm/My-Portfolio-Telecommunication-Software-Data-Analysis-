# 🛒 Market Link - B2B Supply Chain & Ordering Platform

**Market Link** is a high-scale B2B mobile ecosystem that digitizes the supply chain in Iraq. It empowers retail store owners to browse, compare, and order stock directly from major distribution companies through a seamless mobile experience.

---

## 📈 Proven Scale
* **1,000+ Active Markets:** Trusted by over a thousand retail stores for their daily procurement.
* **20+ Distribution Companies:** Partnered with twenty major suppliers to provide a diverse catalog of goods.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📦 Smart Ordering System
* **Direct-to-Company:** Eliminate the need for middle-men or waiting for sales reps. Order directly from company catalogs 24/7.
* **Live Catalog:** Real-time updates on product availability, pricing, and new arrivals from all 20+ partner companies.
* **Order Tracking:** Full visibility into the status of orders, from "Confirmed" to "Out for Delivery."

### 📊 Business Management
* **Order History:** Detailed logs of all previous purchases for better inventory planning and accounting.
* **Digital Invoicing:** Receive and store digital invoices for every transaction, reducing paperwork.
* **Promotions & Offers:** Direct access to exclusive discounts and bulk-buy offers from distribution companies.

### ⚡ Technical Excellence
* **High Performance:** Optimized to handle high traffic and large product databases across 1,000+ simultaneous users.
* **User-Centric Design:** Simple, intuitive interface designed for fast-paced retail environments.
* **Multi-Platform:** Native-level performance on both Android and iOS developed via Flutter.
