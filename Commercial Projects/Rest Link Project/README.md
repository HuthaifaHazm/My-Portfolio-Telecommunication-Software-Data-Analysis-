## 🚀 Key Modules & Features

### 🏢 Core Operations & Sales
* **Hybrid Connectivity:** Fully functional in both **Online and Offline** modes to ensure zero downtime during internet outages.
* **Order Management:** Specialized workflows for **Cashiers** and **Captains** with support for Dine-in and Takeaway.
* **Table Orchestration:** Real-time visual mapping of table status, usage duration, and customer request timers.
* **Payment Versatility:** Integrated support for Cash and Card payments with multi-currency handling.
* **Multilingual Interface:** Fully localized in **English, Arabic, and Kurdish**.

### 🍳 Kitchen & Service Automation
* **KDS (Kitchen Display System):** Digital management of orders in the kitchen to replace paper clutter.
* **Smart Printing:** Advanced routing logic—orders are automatically sent to specific kitchen printers based on the meal type. 
* **Central Monitoring:** A master printer/view for supervisors to track the status of all active orders.

### 📦 Inventory & Warehouse Management
* **Recipe Costing:** Automatically calculates and deducts raw ingredients from the warehouse for every meal sold.
* **Low-Stock Intelligence:** Automated alarms and notifications when inventory levels drop below defined thresholds.
* **Supplier Integration:** Manage purchase invoices, supplier profiles, and procurement history.

### 💰 Finance & HR
* **Financial Suite:** Track all expenses, debts, and payments in one centralized ledger.
* **Human Resources:** Manage employee profiles, attendance, and automated salary calculations.
* **Smart Reporting:** Advanced filtering engine allowing users to extract deep-dive data on any business metric.

### 🔐 Security & Management
* **Granular Permissions:** Total control over user access; permissions can be toggled for individual buttons and clicks (Cashier, Captain, Kitchen, Store).
* **Customer Self-Service:** Digital menus for customers, including direct-ordering capabilities.
* **Owner’s Mobile App:** A dedicated Flutter-based application for owners to monitor real-time dashboards and financial health from anywhere.
